PhoneNap

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

PhoneNap (“the App”) helps parents put a phone to sleep and start real-world play. This policy explains what the App does—and does not—do with data.

Data we collect

  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs in version 1.0.0.
  • Parent PIN and app settings are stored only on your device (secure on-device storage).
  • Optional check-in / feedback notes stay on your device unless you choose to share or copy them yourself.
  • We do not use the microphone, camera, or motion sensors to monitor a child.
  • We do not sell personal data or use advertising IDs.

Children

The App is meant for parents. A child may see sleep or mission screens while a parent uses device features such as Screen Pinning (Android) or Guided Access (iOS). We do not create child accounts, and there is no nap backend that collects information from children.

Third parties

App stores (Google Play / Apple) may process install and purchase data under their own policies. We do not share app content or PIN data with third parties.

Your choices

Uninstall the App to remove most on-device data. On some devices, Keychain or other secure storage items may persist after reinstall until cleared by the operating system. You can also delete the local feedback log from Settings in the App.

Contact

Questions about this policy: github.com/myenimireddy/PhoneNap/issues.
